## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS (user-confirmed, no build step). Files open directly in the browser and deploy to any static host (Netlify, Vercel, GitHub Pages, Hostinger).

## Users

- People in Franca/SP looking after their skin, above all those dealing with melasma (the condition most named in the clinic's reviews), who want a professional they can trust before starting any treatment.
- Inferred from the brief, not confirmed: most arrive from Google Search or the Google Maps listing on a phone, check the 5.0 rating and reviews, and decide whether to message on WhatsApp.
- Review authors quoted in the brief are women (Viviane, Winnie); the Maps listing is marked LGBTQ+ friendly. Do not narrow the audience beyond that.

## Product Purpose

A landing page for Clínica Especializada Dra. Franciela Costa, which has no website today. It turns a visit into a booked aesthetic consultation through WhatsApp and gives the address and hours for the visit. Success = a WhatsApp conversation started (primary) or a route to the clinic opened (secondary).

## Positioning

The owner's own words on Maps: "A consulta estética é um divisor entre um tratamento personalizado, onde o intuito é proporcionar resultado e qualidade no seu tratamento." The consultation comes before any treatment, and treatment is planned for the person. The proof a neighboring clinic cannot copy is her own record: 5.0 on Google from 97 reviews, patients who call her "Fran", and reviews that keep naming skin (pele, 14), treatment (tratamento, 9), result (resultado, 8), and melasma (7).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Offered: aesthetic consultation (consulta estética) and skin treatment, with melasma as the most visible focus. That is the full confirmed scope.
- Not found, never invent: list of services or procedures, prices, before/after results, numbers of patients treated, Instagram handle, logo, brand colors.
- Professional background (user-confirmed): keep neutral, "Dra. Franciela Costa" only. No profession, specialty, or council registration (CRM/CRBM/etc.) until the client confirms; leave a marked slot for it.
- Photos (user-confirmed): none available yet. The Maps listing has 36 photos that need the client's authorization. The page must work without photos and carry one or two marked slots for real ones.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Clínica Especializada Dra. Franciela Costa. Patients call her "Fran" (use it where warmth fits, next to the full name).
- Voice: clinical and welcoming (from the brief). Primary call to action: "Agendar consulta no WhatsApp" (from the brief).
- Anti-reference (user-confirmed): it must not look like a generic luxury spa (gold, marble, empty lines like "sua melhor versão" that fit any clinic).

## Product Principles

1. Consultation first: the page sells the evaluation that precedes any treatment, never a menu of procedures.
2. Only verifiable proof: rating, review count, and quotes exactly as they appear on Maps, attributed.
3. One tap to Fran: WhatsApp stays reachable from every scroll position, above all on a phone.
4. Warm precision: clinical accuracy in what is said, human warmth in how it is said.
